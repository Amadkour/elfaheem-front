# Live rooms — `lib/features/shared/live/`

This file is loaded when working in this folder; the app-wide rules are in the root `CLAUDE.md`. The room itself (control bar, media, sharing, drawing): `lib/features/teacher/sessions/live/CLAUDE.md`.

**Live rooms: share link · lobby · meeting recordings (`lib/features/shared/live/`, spec `docs/backend/live-rooms-spec.md`, D-058)** — A live room is a course session or a calendar meeting (`LiveRoomRef(kind, id)` in `live_room.dart`; `JoinArgs.roomRef`, `isHost`). **The pre-join page never joins by itself (D-106):** every entry (Go live, Live now, Meet now, the schedule, guest links, Rejoin) stops on it until the person presses Join, the page's focused primary button (Enter on the web); `JoinArgs.autoJoin` is deprecated and ignored — don't pass it. **Words follow the kind (D-106):** a meeting says «اجتماع» / "meeting", a course session «الحصة» / "session" — `live_room_words.dart` (`liveTr(key, kind)` reads `<key>_meeting` from translation.json, `liveT(kind, ar:, en:, meetingAr:, meetingEn:)`, `liveRoleLabel`, `liveRoomNoun`; RoomPage sets `LiveRoomWords.current` so leaf texts follow the open room). A new room text that names the room gets its meeting variant with its own Arabic grammar (masculine «الاجتماع»). Tests: `test/org/live_room_words_test.dart`, `test/ui/prejoin_kind_layout_test.dart`. Every join (`…/join`, the public `/public/live/{token}/join`, the signed-in `POST /live/links/{token}/join`) may answer 202 `status: waiting` — `JoinOutcome.parse` tells it apart, `PreJoinPage` then shows `WaitingForHostPage` and polls `/live/requests/{id}` (or the public poll with the secret) until admitted. The host's `LiveLobbyController` (`live_lobby_sheets.dart`) polls `GET /live/{kind}/{id}/lobby` every 15 s in the room (4 s while `LobbySheet` is open, nothing in the background; intervals are constructor parameters), at once on the silent push `live_join_request` (plus one follow-up 4 s later) and on resume; `LobbySheet` admits / rejects and holds the two switches (`admission` open / approve for members, `guest_admission` approve / auto), `ShareLinkSheet` is the meeting's guest link, the notification deep link is `live_lobby`. Meetings (never course sessions) record to the creator's Google Drive / OneDrive: `meeting_recording.dart` + `meeting_recording_sheets.dart` (`RecordSetupSheet` destination + quality before the recorder starts, chunks to `POST /live/recordings/{rid}/chunk`, `RecordingStatusCard` in the meeting detail, `CloudDrivesPage` in settings, `GET /me/cloud` + OAuth in the browser). The participant tile is `ParticipantTileLayout` / `LiveAvatar` / `RecordingPlaceholderFrame` (`widgets/participant.dart`, `live_avatar.dart`, `recording_placeholder.dart`, plain values; the photo comes from the LiveKit token's `metadata.image_url`). app tests `test/org/live_room_test.dart`, `test/org/meeting_recording_test.dart`, `test/ui/live_*_layout_test.dart`, `test/ui/meeting_recording_layout_test.dart`. **Minimize (D-059):** the room is not a route — `PreJoinPage` opens it through `LiveRoomHost.open` into `LiveRoomLayer` (`live_room_host.dart`, in `MaterialApp.builder` around the Navigator), which draws it full-screen in its own nested Navigator (the room's dialogs land there) or, after the Minimize chip / the back button, as a draggable box (`LiveMiniTile` / `LiveMiniBoxLayout` in `live_mini_box.dart`: screen share, else a camera, else an avatar; mute and hang up from the box) while the page stays mounted offstage and the person uses the app. `LiveRoomScope.maybeOf(context)` is null when the room was pushed as a plain route (the fallback `_showRoom` keeps);
