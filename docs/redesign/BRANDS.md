# Two products, one design (D-1810)

The redesign is shared code. What differs between the two products is **data**: the theme the server sends
(`GET /api/v1/theme/{platform}`), the theme defaults in the app, and the platform settings (country, currency, payment
gateway, time zone). Widgets read roles from `context.colors` and the area tokens; they never name a product.

## NextBrain palette (from the logo)

Sampled from `assets/images/logo.png` (2026-11-26): the snake and infinity are green `#186848`, the inner arc is gold
`#C08838`. The phase 1 kit derives the full scale from these two seeds and checks contrast (AA for text ≥ 14 px bold
on white and on the tint). Mapping onto the design's roles (DESIGN_SYSTEM.md §1.4):

| Kit role (`kit.brand.*`) | NextBrain | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `ink` | `#0F3F2C` | text on light, hero base, the phone top bar |
| `deep` | `#08261A` | hero end stop, the forward button's text |
| `primary` | `#186848` (tint `#DFEAE5`, ink `#186848`) | links, primary |
| `partner` | `#2FA26C` | gradient partner and focus ring only (never under text) |
| `warm` | `#C08838` (tint `#F6EEE3`, ink `#8C6329`) | next step, warm highlight |
| `hot` | `#996C2D`; button `hot_a` `#CEA264` → `hot_b` `#C08838`, text `#08261A` | the forward button (gold with deep green text) |
| `warm_on_dark` | `#E6D1B1` | the gold kicker and squiggle on a hero: 4.5:1 or more on every stop of the green heroes (logo green 4.54, admin middle `#155C40` 5.35, ink 7.99, deep 10.84) |
| home, me and admin heroes | `hero_a` `#186848` → `hero_mid` `#155C40` → `deep` `#08261A` | start on the logo green, not the partner, so the gold kicker reads on every stop (it was 1.44:1 on `#2FA26C`) |
| kicker on other heroes | the on-hero ink | the gold kicker is drawn only on heroes where it reaches 4.5:1 on every stop (home, me, admin, teacher; `AppHero.kickerInk`); sun, lime, sky, gold and the other light-start heroes draw it in the on-hero ink |
| `page` / `text` / `muted` | `#F5F8F7` / `#102B20` / `#51675E` | 60 % neutral |

These are generated by `tool/redesign/kit_palette.py` (`--check`: 0 under the floor) and copied into
`lib/config/theme/kit_palette_defaults.dart` and the back's `features/platform/theme_kit_defaults.py`; the full tables
are KIT_SPEC.md §1.2. **Mint decision:** the shared mint is moved to hue 174 (`#19C2B1`) in NextBrain, because at its
shared hue it sits 10° from the brand green; lime (75° away) is not moved.

Shared in both products (functional colours, not brand): the six student area accents (sun, mint, grape, pink, lime,
sky), the subject colours, and the status colours (live coral, success mint, warning sun, locked, error). Where an
area accent clashes with NextBrain's green (mint, lime), the kit may shift it, recording the choice here.
